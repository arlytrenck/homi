Homi brand kit

self-hosted / at a glance / yours

Homi is a calm window onto your own infrastructure: home-sized, fast, quiet, and yours. The mark is a house with a status dot at its center. The dot is a service that is alive, and it is the same dot as the i in the wordmark.

Logo variants

App icon (tile)Favicon, README, small sizes. Works on any background.
Mark, dark surfacesTeal #2dd4bf to indigo #6366f1.
Mark, light surfacesDarker gradient. The dark-surface teal is only 1.9:1 on white.
MonoUses currentColor for single-color contexts.
Lockup, lightTile plus wordmark with a darker gradient.
Maskable iconFull-bleed; the mark stays inside the 80% safe circle.

Clear space and size

Clear spaceKeep at least the width of the dot's outer ring (about 1/5 of the mark) free on every side.
Minimum sizeMark 16 px, lockup 96 px wide. Under 24 px use the tile.

Usage

DoPick the variant by background. Use the tile on photos or busy backgrounds. Keep the gradient teal top-left to indigo bottom-right. Write the name in lowercase: homi.
Don'tRecolor, rotate, outline, add glows or shadows, stretch, or use the dark-surface gradient on white.
